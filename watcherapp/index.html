What you are agreeing to

  • You hold 1 of n shares

    The principal splits the vault key into n pieces. You get one, sealed to your key.

  • Alone you reveal nothing

    One share says nothing about the dossier. It takes t watchers acting independently.

  • Keep this running

    Your watcher has to be online to see heartbeats stop and to post your share to the public release address.

  • You can stop at any time

    Stopping is always your call. But if too few watchers are running, a release fails, and that may be because of you.
