Threat model, honestly

What holds, and what does not yet.

Holds

  • Seize two of five watchers: the switch still fires.
  • Two watchers collude: not one bit leaks.
  • Seize the server: there is none.
  • Force a check-in: duress releases instead.
  • Replay an old heartbeat: sequence numbers reject it.
  • A watcher goes offline: it catches up from the chain.

Does not yet

  • Three watchers collude: early release. Choose them from different jurisdictions.
  • The principal’s signing key is seized: heartbeats can be forged.
  • Heartbeat timing is visible to a watcher, though amounts and memos are not.
  • Cancel cannot recall a share already posted.
  • Testnet prototype, not audited.
Judges respect a threat model more than a feature list. The seized-key case is the real open problem; a hardware key or a second co-signer for cancel is the obvious next step.
