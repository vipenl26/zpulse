Why today’s switches fail

Every existing design has a throat to choke.

A server

Can be seized or shut down.

Its operator can be ordered, bribed or threatened.

A recipient

Can be pressured or silenced.

One person holding the file is one person to lean on.

A check-in

Can be forced at gunpoint.

“Log in and press the button” is exactly what a captor asks for.

Three failure modes, three things we refuse to have: no server, no single recipient, and a check-in that can be coerced without consequence. Each principle on the next slides removes one of these.
