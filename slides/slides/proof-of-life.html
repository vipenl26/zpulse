Proof of life must be unforgeable, and cheap.

Every 48 hours, one shielded Zcash transaction

ZP1 HB vault seq ts sig

Five outputs, one per watcher, a few thousand zatoshi each, the same signed memo in all of them.

Unforgeable: Ed25519 signature over the sequence number. A captor sending coins to the address proves nothing.

Cheap: about 0.001 ZEC per heartbeat.

Useful: the payment funds the watcher’s own release transaction later.

The heartbeat is a payment. That's the trick we borrowed from Paid Private File: a viewing key is a smart escrow. Each watcher gets a diversified address made for this vault, so a payment matches exactly one vault, and the watcher reads it with its own wallet. The coins that arrive are what the watcher later spends to post its share.
