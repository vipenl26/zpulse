Why Zcash

Shielded memos are the only transport.

512 bytes, encrypted, on-chain

A signed heartbeat or a Shamir share fits in one memo. Sender, amount and memo are hidden from everyone but the holder of the viewing key.

Viewing key as smart escrow

No contract decides a payment happened. A light client with the right key sees it. Borrowed from Paid Private File.

Diversified addresses

One fresh address per vault per watcher, so a payment maps to exactly one vault. Nothing links the five watchers.

No smart contract, no server, no relay. Threshold keepers and memos-as-transport come from z-canary; we removed the recipient and made the heartbeat pay.

Credit where due: Paid Private File for the viewing-key escrow and per-order diversified addresses, z-canary for threshold keepers on memos. Our additions: release to the world, heartbeats that fund the release, duress, and no peer network at all.
