Duress

“Check in, or else.”

She checks in. The transaction looks identical. It is signed with a second key. To the watchers it means: release now.

The one check-in a captor can force is the one that burns them.

The accent slide. Same amount, same address, same memo shape; only the signing key differs, and only the watchers know there are two keys. This removes the third failure mode: a coerced check-in.
