Encrypt the file once. Split the key into 5 shares. Any 3 rebuild it. Fewer than 3 reveal nothing, not a single bit.

Two watchers seized: the switch still fires. Two watchers colluding: nothing leaks. Shamir, 1979. Pure math, no trust.

Shamir secret sharing. The key is a point on a polynomial; each watcher gets one evaluation. With fewer than the threshold you learn nothing, mathematically. This one idea removes the single recipient and makes the switch survive seizure of a minority of watchers.
