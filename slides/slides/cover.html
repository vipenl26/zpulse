zpulse · a dead man’s switch on Zcash

Open on the quote. Let it sit for a beat. Hughes wrote this in 1993; thirty-three years later the tools finally exist to make it mechanical rather than a plea. Then: this is zpulse, a dead man's switch for whistleblowers, built on Zcash in one day.
