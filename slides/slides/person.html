First principles · 0

A journalist holds a file.

Her only real protection is a promise: if anything happens to her, the file comes out.

A dead man’s switch is that promise, made mechanical.

The whole design question: who do you have to trust to keep the promise?

Start from the human. The file is not the asset; the credible threat of its release is. A dead man's switch turns a promise into a mechanism. Everything that follows is about removing people you'd otherwise have to trust.
