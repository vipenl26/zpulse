Four ways the pulse plays out

A principal holds a dossier encrypted with key K. K is split into 5 Shamir shares, threshold 3, one sealed to each watcher. Heartbeats and shares travel only as shielded Zcash memos. No server, no relay, and the watchers never talk to each other.

Rules the animation follows

A heartbeat is one transaction with five outputs. Each watcher sees only its own output, on an address made for this vault.

A watcher's deadline is the last heartbeat's block time plus interval plus grace. Past it, the watcher posts its share to the release address, alone.

The release address's viewing key is public. Any 3 shares rebuild K. One or two shares reveal nothing.

Timeline is deterministic: Restart replays the exact same sequence. Times are demo seconds. Link a scenario with #heartbeat, #two-down, #five-release or #three-release.
